CAPRICORN BLINDS

Manufacturing Simulator

Run the Capricorn Blinds factory. Take orders, cut fabric to size on the precision bench, assemble blinds, ship them out and grow your empire — one window at a time.

A 2D tycoon game · v1.0

How to Play

  1. Take an order from the Orders panel. Each order needs a blind type, colour and a set width.
  2. Stock fabric in the right colour from the Shop. Each blind built uses one unit of matching fabric.
  3. Cut & Build at the bench. A blade sweeps across the ruler — click (or press Space) when it lands inside the green target zone for a clean cut. Perfect hits give bonus cash & reputation.
  4. Complete orders before their deadline (in days) to get paid. Missed orders cost you reputation.
  5. End the day to advance time. You pay rent each day and new orders arrive. Spend profits on Upgrades to build faster and better.
  6. Keep cash above zero and reputation healthy. Go bankrupt and it's game over!
